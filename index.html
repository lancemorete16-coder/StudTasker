Privacy policy

What StudTasker stores

Your tasks (title, description, subject, due date and time, priority, completion status), your list of subjects, and preferences (dark mode, study hours per day). Please don’t enter passwords, ID numbers, or other sensitive information.

Why it is stored

To show your tasks, deadlines, dashboard, calendar, and assistant suggestions after you refresh or reopen the page.

How Local Storage works

Your browser keeps this data on your device, tied to this site and this browser profile. It does not sync to other devices or browsers, and there is no account or backup.

External AI services

None. LanceBot is a basic rule-based tool that runs in your browser using your saved tasks. No task data is sent to an AI service by this app.

Third-party services

StudTasker uses no analytics, ads, or third-party libraries. The website host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) as part of serving the page.

Clearing your data

Use Settings, then “Clear all data”. You can also clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

Limits of privacy and security

  • Local Storage is not encrypted.
  • Anyone using the same browser profile on this device can read it.
  • Browser extensions or shared computers can expose it.
  • Clearing browser data permanently deletes your tasks.
